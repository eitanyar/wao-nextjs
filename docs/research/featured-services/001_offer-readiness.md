## Policy operating boundaries

- Google says lead-generation agents or companies are not eligible for a Business Profile. Only owners or authorized representatives may manage one; owner consent and direct owner participation in verification are required, and the profile website must be the single authoritative website owned and managed by the business owner. Sources: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en and https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en (accessed 2026-09-05).
- Google third-party policy requires written or positive-action consent, client ownership or co-ownership, change transparency, management-fee disclosure, a discoverable third-party notice, separable reporting, easy termination, and release of permissions within seven business days. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7353941 (accessed 2026-09-05).
- Therefore, never create or pre-claim a GBP for an incubated lead-generation property. For GBP remediation plus rebuild, the business owner remains owner/co-owner and WAO acts only as manager/authorized representative.
- Positive authority metrics cannot establish an expired domain as safe. Require archived-content review, trademark/name-confusion review, backlink/penalty/abuse review, registration-chain evidence, and manual legal due diligence before acquisition or transfer.
- ICANN UDRP supports this boundary: https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/consensus-policies/uniform-domain-name-dispute-resolution-policy/uniform-domain-name-dispute-resolution-policy-25-02-2012-en (accessed 2026-09-05).
