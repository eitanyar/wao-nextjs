## Remaining material findings

1. **An unsupported conversation-duration promise returned.** Page 3, line 389 says `בשיחה קצרה` (“in a short conversation”), while the correction contract and v2 ledger explicitly say no duration/timing promises. The prior draft correctly removed the free 30-minute consultation, but this still promises a duration-like experience without supporting evidence. Remove the duration characterization; keep the CTA to a plain conversation/contact action.

2. **The Ads page implies lead outcomes and click-fraud protection beyond the recorded evidence.** Page 3, line 327 calls the five steps ones “that bring real inquiries”; lines 306 and 366 also present inquiries as a direct expected result. More concretely, line 346 says there is protection against click fraud, while the packet's own ledger at line 468 says the tooling/vendor claim needs revalidation. The source anchors describe Ads as a target/deferred offer, not verified live operation (v2 lines 443–445). Do not present unverified protection or assured lead outcomes as established facts; bound the language to the evidenced service/measurement setup and retain revalidation as a publication gate.

3. **The cancellation FAQ is evasive on a material unresolved term.** Page 2, lines 268–269 asks what happens to the site if the customer stops, then says only that a full answer will be given in conversation. The same packet (lines 441 and 468) confirms the site-fate/cancellation policy is unresolved, and the source explicitly parks that decision as open (pivot lines 182–186). This avoids inventing a policy, correctly, but leaves a directly asked, consequential buyer question unanswered and weakens the promised helpfulness/trust. The copy should candidly identify that the policy must be clarified before commitment rather than imply the conversation already supplies a settled “full answer.” No policy should be invented by the reviewer.
