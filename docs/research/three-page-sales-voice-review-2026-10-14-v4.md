## Material finding

1. **A residual discovery certainty claim remains.** The homepage final heading says `הלקוח הבא שלך כבר מחפש אותך בגוגל.` (1.10, line 134). Although the following sentence changes the earlier `נוודא שהוא ימצא` assurance to enabling presence, the opening sentence still asserts that a specific next customer is already searching for this business. In context, that reads as a factual certainty about discovery demand, not merely a desired outcome. The governing contract permits aspirational benefit language but requires removing wording that promises guaranteed discovery or inquiries. Reframe this assertion as an aspiration or possibility; no other certainty-level defect comparable to the v3 findings was material on this full read.
