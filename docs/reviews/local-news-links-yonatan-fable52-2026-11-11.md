# Local news placements: ranking effect only — Yonatan, 2026-11-11

## 1. Do the winners have local news links?

**What I could verify (fetched today, free methods only):**

- **Homepages of 31 Set A/B/C winners:** none shows an "as seen in" block or links to a local news site. One (mrplumber.co.il) links to a Maariv item under a "בתקשורת" heading.
- **Gal-Gefen:** I sampled 120 recent articles from its "עסקים מומלצים" and "צרכנות" sections for Rishon, Holon, Bat Yam, Rehovot and Nes Ziona.
  - 29 carried an external link, and all 38 such links were followed (no `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`).
  - None was for a plumber, electrician, locksmith, AC or moving business; buyers were restaurants, retail and a law firm.
  - None of the 54 winner domains appeared.
- **Other local or "news" sites I could reach** (news08.net, kan-ashkelon.co.il, ashdodnet, b7net, jerusalemnet): searching their archives for trade terms turned up placed trade articles.
  - All 25 trade-related outbound links I found were followed, with keyword anchors such as "אינסטלטור במודיעין" and "הובלות בחיפה".
  - Five winner domains appeared: piper.co.il, electplus.co.il, moving-israel.co.il (on two sites), bigplumber.co.il.

**What I could not verify:**

- Any site's full backlink profile, which needs a paid tool. "No news link found" means not found in my sample, not that none exists.
- Whether any specific article was paid; the "תוכן שיווקי" labels I detected may sit in the site template rather than on the article.
- mynet, Hashikma, Melabes, Kol Ha'ir and Haipo, which blocked the fetch.
- Web searches for the two strongest plumbers' brand names found no press at all, but that index is not a backlink source.

**Reading (judgment):** placed articles on portal-type sites are a known tactic among the larger lead-gen players. In this sample they are not what separates the winners, and I saw no home-service trade using Gal-Gefen.

## 2. Ranking effect by link type

Established first:

- **Policy:** Google lists "advertorials or native advertising where payment is received for articles that include links that pass ranking credit" as link spam. Paid links are fine when marked `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow` (spam policies, read 2026-11-11).
- **Marked links:** Google says links with these attributes "will generally not be followed" (outbound-links doc, read 2026-11-11).
- **Local Pack:** Google's prominence factor is "based on info like how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have" (GBP help, read 2026-11-11).
- **Survey weight:** in Whitespark 2026, link factors rank #3, #5 and #13 for local organic, but link quantity ranks #56 for the Local Pack.

The table below is my judgment.

| Form | (a) Organic "service + city" / "trade + city" | (b) Local Pack / GBP |
|---|---|---|
| Paid, marked `sponsored`/`nofollow` | Near zero direct effect. Indirect value only: readers, branded searches, a mention. | Near zero as a link; small as a local mention of name and city. |
| Paid, link left followed | Works if undetected: likely the strongest single link a new site has. It violates policy. Realistic risk for one small site is that Google silently ignores the link (money wasted), not a penalty. The larger risk is ours: the same portals, cadence and anchors repeated across every client is a detectable pattern. | Small positive, same caveats. |
| Earned editorial or unlinked mention | Best case and fully safe. A real story with a link is worth more than several placed ones. Unlinked mentions add little to organic. | Modest positive for prominence. |

Note the practical point: Gal-Gefen's default appears to be followed links. A compliant placement means WAO must ask for `rel="sponsored"`, which removes most of the ranking value we would be paying for.

## 3. On-site depth alone vs links (judgment)

| Query class | With the ~30-page model and only the citation floor | What links add |
|---|---|---|
| Problem / question + city | Rankable without links. | Little. |
| "Service + city", small or mid-size city, niche service | Positions 5–15; some top 5. This is where depth alone pays. | Moves page-2 results onto page 1. |
| "Service + city", competitive (drain opening, leak detection in large cities) | Stalls around 8–20 against 100–700-URL sites. | This is the ceiling links break. |
| "Trade + city" head | Stalls around 12–25. | Needed for top 10; not sufficient alone. |
| Neighbouring towns | Works in small towns. | Helps in larger ones. |

I cannot measure how much of the winners' lead comes from links versus size and age, since I have no backlink data.

## 4. Cadence and quantity

- **Six placements a year is small but not nothing** for a site starting with zero links. Hebrew local sites in these SERPs likely have few quality referring domains (unmeasured).
- **Repeating the same paper adds little.** The second and third link from one domain is worth far less than the first. One domain per client per year captures most of the value.
- **A fixed bimonthly rhythm with keyword anchors is the pattern to avoid.** Vary timing, use brand or plain-URL anchors.
- **Order of value:** the citation floor (GBP, b144, easy, d.co.il, midrag, suppliers) comes first and is close to free. A news placement is step two, not a substitute.

## 5. Verdict

1. **As a ranking product, a compliant sponsored placement does not earn its place.** Marked `sponsored`, it does almost nothing for blue links; left followed, it works only by breaking policy across our whole client fleet.
2. **It can earn a place as a publicity and proof add-on,** sold honestly as local exposure with any ranking effect treated as a bonus. At most one or two distinct local outlets per client per year, brand anchor, pointing to the homepage (the GBP landing page), not a service page.
3. **For bottom-of-funnel "service + city" leads, on-site depth plus the citation floor comes first.** Add earned mentions (a real local story, a sponsorship, a supplier page) only for clients stuck at positions 8–20 after month 6.
