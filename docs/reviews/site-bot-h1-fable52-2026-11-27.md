## 1. The H1

**קידום עסק בגוגל עם מטרה אחת: שהטלפון שלך יצלצל**

## 2. Runners-up

- **שמי שמחפש בעל מקצוע בגוגל יתקשר דווקא אליך** — the purest dream line, but it lost because it repeats the subhead's first sentence almost word for word and leaves the keyword only on the small pill.
- **קידום עסק בגוגל — שיתקשרו אליך, ולא למתחרה** — it lost because it sells through the competitor rather than through his own phone, it repeats «מתקשר» from the subhead, and the subhead already delivers the "he picks you" beat.

## 3. Why the winner is best

- **Goal vs means:** the H1 names the category in plain Hebrew and then the one thing he is buying, a ringing phone. The site, profile and reviews drop to the badge and subhead as the means, which is what Eitan asked for.
- **Keyword:** the H1 carries «קידום עסק בגוגל» exactly, matching the title and nav. It is the bigger and easier term, and the page has no traffic to protect.
- **«בניית אתר לעסק קטן» leaves the H1:** carrying both makes a 14+ word headline that leads with the means. The title keeps «אתר לעסק קטן», and Yonatan can anchor the full phrase in the site section («שימצאו אותך גם כשמחפשים…»).
- **Claim safety:** «עם מטרה אחת» states a purpose, so it promises neither a position nor a number of calls, yet it still reads bold.
- **Fit:** it is 9 words, ends in Hebrew, and repeats nothing in the subhead's first sentence. «יצלצל» sets up the subhead's closing «ומתקשר» without duplicating it.

## 4. Badge and subhead

The badge becomes redundant, since it would show the same keyword directly above the H1. Replace it with what he gets:

**אתר, פרופיל וביקורות · בלי פרסום ממומן**

This mirrors the second half of the title. It does share «אתר» and «ביקורות» with the subhead's first sentence; I kept that on purpose, because the badge names the three parts and the subhead shows why those three.

The subhead stays as is.
