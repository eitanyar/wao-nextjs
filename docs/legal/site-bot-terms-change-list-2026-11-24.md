# Site Bot terms: change list for the lawyer (2026-11-24)

Source: the current terms, `docs/legal/site-bot-terms-of-use.md` v1.2 (17.11.2026). Decisions by Eitan on 2026-11-24,
following `docs/reviews/site-bot-mvp-hard-audit-2026-11-24.md` and `docs/reviews/site-bot-mvp-audit-review-fable52-2026-11-24.md`.
Intent only. The Hebrew wording comes from the lawyer and waocopy.

| § | Today | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §5 permissions | "No connection to … Google Search Console" | Allow an optional, read-only Search Console connection that the owner grants for his site, used for the monthly report and indexing checks. | The monthly report and the go-live checks need it. Today the terms forbid it. |
| §5 permissions | GBP: manager access "by the owner's invitation" | Add: (a) WAO stores proof of his written or digital consent; (b) every change WAO makes to his profile is logged and reported to him; (c) WAO never changes his business name, and changes phone, address or categories only after asking him first; (d) a link to Google's "Working with a third party" notice. | Google's Business Profile third-party policies (fetched 2026-11-24): consent must be written or a positive action, the customer must be informed of all changes, and the disclosure notice must be shared. |
| §6 domain | "If you have your own domain, the site goes up on it from the start, within three business days" | Narrow it: the domain is always in the client's name. We put a site on a domain only if no site is live on it today. Replacing an existing site, on the same domain only with 1:1 redirects, is a separate, scoped service. | Eitan's policy: never put an existing site at risk. Today's wording promises an attach that the policy forbids. |
| §8 exit | Site offline 30 days after the last paid month; full copy free after 12 paid months, ₪900+VAT before | Add the **business-card exit**: at the offline date the address shows a free one-page card (name, phone, hours, Google profile link) for 12 months, and all old pages redirect to it. Before manager access ends, WAO sets the profile's website field to the owner's choice (the card, blank, or his own new URL). His photos, recordings, facts and leads are always his, free. Full site restored if he returns within 90 days. Keep the 12-month / ₪900 full-copy rule. | Approved by Eitan 2026-11-24. His Google profile never points at a dead page, and the living services stay the reason to subscribe. |
| §15 IP | Site texts and the copy are subject to §8 | Add: the owner's own material (photos, recordings, transcripts, facts he supplied) is his. Only the system and the generated full site are covered by §8. | Follows the §8 change. |
| §18 termination | Termination removes WAO's manager access | Reorder: first set the profile website field (per §8), then remove access. | Otherwise we can't fix the link after access is gone. |
| §2 service | "A person at WAO checks the site before launch" | Add: going live needs the owner's intake material (a short recorded conversation or voice note and at least 3 real photos) and his confirmation of the facts list. A client who supplies none gets a smaller site (home + services page). | Depth intake approved as required, 2026-11-24. It also explains timing, against the one-business-day promise in §6. |
| §6 timing | Subdomain site "within one business day of payment" | Count it from receipt of the intake material and the fact confirmation, not from payment. | The depth intake makes "one day from payment" impossible to keep. |
| §2 + §5 review replies | "suggestions for replies" to reviews; GBP row: "reply to reviews and publish updates" | Remove draft review replies and posting profile updates until they are built. Keep review requests. | Eitan 2026-11-24 ("go"): the line comes off the page and the terms until it's built (truth check `docs/reviews/site-bot-product-truth-check-2026-11-24.md`). |
| §2 monthly report | "a monthly report" | Keep. For December it's prepared by hand from Google's dashboard; automated v0 by 20.12. | Eitan 2026-11-24 ("go"). |
