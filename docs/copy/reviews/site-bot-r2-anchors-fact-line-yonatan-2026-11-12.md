# /site-bot round 2: fact line + anchor rules (Yonatan, 2026-11-12)

## Fact line (verified 2026-11-12)
Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and advises complete, accurate business info and more reviews. The leading 2026 industry survey ranks a dedicated page for each service #1 for local organic results (#24 for map results).
- Copy MAY say: it "helps Google understand and show you for each service"; same services, areas and hours on site and profile = Google's "complete and accurate info".
- Copy must NEVER say: "you'll rank", "first place", or that site-to-profile matching is a ranking factor by itself.
- Confidence: high on direction, medium on rankings (Whitespark is a practitioner survey).
- Sources: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 · https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/ (2025-11-06)

## h1 rule
- "בניית אתר לעסק קטן" byte-exact as the first words, nothing inserted inside it.
- Names all three parts in plain words (site, Google profile, reviews) plus the "one system" idea. Widen the site, never shrink it.
- ≤90 chars; verb gender agrees with "בנייה".
- Forbidden: negating or shrinking the site ("only a third", "not a website", "not just a site builder"); Latin tokens (GBP, SEO); prices; ranking promises; "בונה אתרים".

## Title rule
- Head term first; ≤60 chars incl. "‏ | WAO"; the remaining ~35 chars carry the three-part idea; "גוגל" in Hebrew; ends on a Hebrew word before the RLM suffix; no price, no Latin token, no ranking promise. OG title identical.

## Badge
Not an anchor; may carry the sharper "a site alone isn't enough" angle.
