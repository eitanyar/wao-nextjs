## Creative brief
**Reader.** A tradesman who paid for clicks or an agency and saw no work from it, or who fears burning money. Solution-aware, has heard every agency promise.

**Big idea.** Google ads that learn from the jobs you close, not from clicks. Run by a bot for ₪249 a month instead of an agency retainer, in an account that is yours.

**Eitan's point, said flat as an opinion about the category:** cheap management chases clicks. We chase the jobs he closes.

**Hero.** Develop the kept h1. Subhead: the kept two sentences, then the agency contrast in one clause ending on our price "a month". Badge: the price. Primary button: the free 30-minute call with Eitan; secondary: the no-cost demo.

**Flow.**
1. **The burned scene.** One dated moment, stated as fact, no "maybe": the money went out, the clicks came in, the phone stayed quiet, and then they told him he needs more budget or more time.
2. **The mechanism.** Exactly three moments of the learning loop, each with a day of his week and what he gains: an enquiry lands in his list; he marks it relevant or junk, closed or not; Google learns which searches bring work and the budget moves there. Nothing else lives here.
3. **What the bot does for you.** The only home for: building the account, ads and landing pages from zero; the budget guard; the weekly report; Eitan reachable by name.
4. **Proof.** The no-cost demo; Google Partner with its one fact; Eitan by name and role. The credit is NOT here.
5. **The offer, argued.** ₪249 against the agency anchor; the ₪1,500 minimum as his money going into ads, not to us; the Google credit as a bonus we bring, one sentence, one qualifier (Google decides); the refund written as pride, its conditions in one plain line a tradesman parses at a glance.
6. **Yours, free to leave.** Stated as pride. Exit mechanics are FAQ only.
7. **How it starts.** Three real actions.
8. **FAQ.**
9. **Close.** Returns to jobs, not clicks.

**Objections to answer in the body:** "I already tried and it didn't work." "A bot instead of a person?" "₪1,500 is a lot." "I'll get locked in." "How do I know it's working?"

**FAQ topics (questions in his voice; answer opens with what he gets):** who is paid what; "How do I know it will bring me work?"; Google's payment and ID checks; the refund in full; cancellation and landing pages; "Do you fix my existing account?"; "Am I eligible for the Google credit?"; ads versus organic; "When do I pay?".

## Facts you may state (checkable claims come ONLY from here)
- Price: ₪249 + VAT a month. Write prices as ₪249, ₪1,500, ₪900 throughout.
- Payment starts at signup. (FAQ only.)
- Ad budget: at least ₪1,500 a month, about ₪50 a day, paid by him directly to Google from his own Google Ads account.
- Agency anchor: agency management typically starts at about ₪1,500 a month + VAT, with the ad budget on top. Allowed: about six times our fee, and that is where agency prices start. Category only, never a competitor name.
- We open a new Google Ads account in his name and build campaigns, ads and landing pages from zero, starting with his main service. We never take over or fix an existing account. The free call can look at the ads he runs today.
- Every enquiry from the landing page, by form, call or WhatsApp click, lands in his leads list. He marks each one relevant or not, and closed or not. That marking goes back to Google, which learns which searches bring jobs.
- The bot cuts searches that spend money without bringing enquiries. A cost-per-enquiry ceiling stops spend from running away. Fake-click blocking is included.
- Weekly report: how many enquiries came in, how many he marked as real, what each one cost in shekels.
- Before anything goes live, he sees the plan (area, budget, keywords, a sample ad) and can ask for changes.
- Google Partner: WAO is a Google Partner. Partner status is not permanent; Google checks daily that the agency still meets its performance, ad-spend and certification requirements.
- Google credit: if his business has never advertised on Google Ads, WAO, as a Google Partner, applies to Google for an ad credit on his new account. Google decides whether to approve and sets the amount and terms. Most owners who open an account alone never hear of it. It is ad money in his Google Ads account, on top of his budget.
- Refund: our ₪249 back, no reason needed, when all three are true: his ad budget was at least ₪1,500 a month during the first 30 days; his business hasn't had this refund before; he asks within 7 days after the 30 days end. The 30 days start the day his first ad shows on Google; days Google holds the account for checks don't count. It covers our fee only.
- If we never get an ad live, he gets the ₪249 back without asking.
- Google checks his payment details, sometimes his ID, before ads go live. He enters them himself. If Google's checks aren't done within 21 days of payment, he can cancel for a full refund. (FAQ only.)
- The account, campaigns and data are his. He can leave any month. After cancelling, the landing pages stay up 30 days; he can buy them for ₪900; free after 12 paid months. (FAQ only.)
- Eitan, WAO's founder, in digital marketing since 2006.
- Must appear in the offer block or FAQ: the fee is separate from the ad budget, which goes directly to Google; it is his own account, linked to WAO's manager account; he can see clicks and cost at any time; there is a written agreement.
