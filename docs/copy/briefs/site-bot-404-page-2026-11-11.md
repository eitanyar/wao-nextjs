# Brief: "page not found" (404) page on Site-Bot client sites (2026-11-11)

**Where it goes:** the 404 page of every generated client website (the website of a local trade business: plumber, electrician, AC technician and the like), served when a visitor opens an address that doesn't exist. **The reader is the business's customer, not the owner.** He probably followed an old link or mistyped. The page already has the business name at the top (links home), a link "חזרה לעמוד הבית" (existing approved copy, keep), and the footer.

**Needed (3 strings):**
1. `title`: the browser-tab title (the page appends " | {business name}" itself; don't include it). Short.
2. `h1`: the heading shown on the page (replaces the bare "404").
3. `line`: one short sentence under the heading that tells him what happened and points him to the homepage link below.

**Constraints:**
- Neutral, calm, plain.
- No jokes and no "oops".
- No apology essay.
- No mention of WAO.
- No business-specific facts, because it's generic across all businesses.
- Address the customer in singular masculine.
- No ASCII quotes.

**Output:** JSON `{"title": "...", "h1": "...", "line": "..."}`.
