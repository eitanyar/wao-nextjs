# Dash v2, A-episodes: the Ads Bot line must sell the benefit (2026-11-12)

The founder wants the product line to lead with what the owner GETS, not how the bot works. Today's lines explain the mechanism ("בוט מודעות לומד רק מהפניות שסימנת כעבודה סגורה…"). His words: "I need the best ads. I can't back up the promise; that's what ads do." So write a confident, aspirational benefit. Direction, in English: "Ads Bot: your ad money goes to jobs, not to clicks" / "more real jobs from Google ads".

Rewrite ONLY the Ads Bot product line in each A-episode, 30s and 15s:
- benefit first;
- native spoken Hebrew, male singular, short;
- it flows out of the story's punchline (A1 the taxi driver who pays passengers, A2 the crowd got fat and the till didn't, A3 a lot of pages for 'no'). A light callback to the story's image is welcome (A1 passengers who actually ride, A2 buyers not tasters, A3 an answer to "and customers?").
- You may keep a short mechanism hint after the benefit if it makes the benefit believable, but the benefit comes first.

**Boundary:** confident puffery is fine ("more jobs", "money goes to jobs"). Not allowed: specific numbers, a guaranteed cost per lead or guaranteed results, "first place" or rankings. Google's ad policy and Israeli consumer law forbid false checkable claims.
