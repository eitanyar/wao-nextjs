## Creative brief
**Reader.** A one-man trade business (plumber, electrician, AC technician). Good at the work, rated well on Google, but no site or a dead one, and a Google profile somebody opened once. Google is where his work comes from. He has heard every "build a website" promise. He doesn't want a website; he wants the customer who searches right now to choose him.

**Big idea.** A customer judges you on three things in the same ten seconds: your Google profile, your site, and what your customers wrote. When all three tell the same story about what you do, she calls you. Site Bot builds and runs all three as one system, every month, for the one-man business that lives on Google.

**Enemy.** The patchwork: a site on its own, a profile on its own, reviews left to luck. Everyone else sells him one piece.

**Hero.**
- h1: opens with בניית אתר לעסק קטן, then widens it: the site comes as part of one system with his Google profile and his reviews. Make the site bigger, never smaller.
- Subhead: the three parts named in one breath, and the gain. Upkeep words (hosting, updates) do not appear in the hero.
- Badge: the price, ₪199 + VAT a month, no commitment.
- Primary button: the free check, which shows him his three stops as a customer sees them today. Secondary: a call with Eitan.
- Line beside the button: the facts about the check below.
- After five seconds he believes: this is made for a one-man business like me, it handles everything that gets me found and chosen on Google, and it costs less than one small job.

**Flow.**
1. **Scene.** The customer's ten seconds, as one concrete moment: she searches, sees the map with stars, reads the reviews, taps the site. He is chosen or skipped before any call.
2. **The claim, in the biggest type.** Three parts, one story.
3. **The site as his tree of services.** A page for each job he does, the way customers search for it. Never a page count.
4. **The Google profile, matched to the site.** Same services, areas and hours. We manage it for him; he stays the owner.
5. **Reviews.** After a job, he gets a ready message to forward to the customer, with the link. When a review comes in, he gets a draft reply to approve.
6. **Every month.** It keeps running, and he gets the monthly report. Hosting and changes by message share one line here, never their own cards.
7. **Alternatives.** Each gives him a single piece: a developer or agency, a site builder, building alone with AI, just a Google profile, doing nothing.
8. **Price.** Three jobs normally bought separately, for one small job's worth.
9. **Proof.** The free check (he sees it on himself), Eitan by name and role, a person at WAO checking every site before it goes live.
10. **FAQ.**
11. **Close.** Returns to three parts, one story.

**Objections to answer in the body:** "A site won't bring me work." "I have no time." "I'm not technical." "I'll get locked in." "Why not Wix?" "My nephew will build it."

**FAQ topics (questions in his voice; answer opens with what he gets):** "What do you actually do so I show up on Google?"; billing and cancelling; one business day versus three, and when the clock starts; the domain; after cancelling; texts written with AI and checked by a person; "Do I have to do anything every month?"; "What's in the monthly report?".

## Facts you may state (checkable claims come ONLY from here)
- ₪199 + VAT a month. No trial, no commitment. Cancel online any time; it takes effect at the end of the paid month. (Mechanics FAQ only.)
- The free check: he types only his business name. No signup. The result comes back in seconds and shows his Google profile the way a customer sees it.
- Live within one business day of payment on a WAO address, or within three business days on his own domain. A person at WAO checks every site before it goes live.
- How it starts: he answers a few questions in a chat. We pull his details from his Google profile, so he doesn't type what Google already knows.
- The site has a page for each service he does, linked from one service tree, built from research into what people near him search for his trade. It shows his opening hours, a call button, a link to his Google profile, and reviews he picks.
- Google's own advice for showing up locally: complete, accurate business details and more reviews. Industry experts rank a dedicated page per service among the top factors for local results. Copy may say it helps Google understand and show him for each service.
- His Google Business Profile, managed for him: we connect as a manager and he stays the owner. We keep its services, areas, hours and description matching the site.
- Reviews: after a job he gets a ready message to forward to his customer with his review link. We never message his customers ourselves. When a review comes in, he gets a draft reply to approve before it is posted.
- Monthly report: how many people called from his Google profile, asked for directions, and clicked to his site, plus his new reviews.
- Updates by sending the bot a message. No editor, no system to learn. Hosting included.
- His domain and his Google profile stay his. After cancelling, the site stays up 30 days; he can buy the site out; free after 12 paid months. (FAQ only.)
- Eitan, WAO's founder, in digital marketing since 2006.
- Launch date: Site Bot opens to new clients on 19.11.2026.
