His direction, in English: **"Site Bot — getting you more jobs and recommendations from Google."** Lead with what he GETS: more work and more recommendations (reviews) from Google.

- native spoken Hebrew, male singular, short;

- In S2, the benefit naturally leans on recommendations (reviews); never imply we write or steer reviews.
- "More jobs" is fine as a benefit. Don't promise rankings or "first place", numbers, or guarantees.
- Plain form "סייט בוט" (a script converts it for the voice). Keep the line's audio tag.

The founder's phrasing is the intent, not the wording. Find the Sabra line that says it best; it can be the same line in all three episodes if that works as a recurring tagline.
