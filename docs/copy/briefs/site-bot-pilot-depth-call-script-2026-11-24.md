# Brief: pilot depth-intake call script + photo request (2026-11-24)

**Situation:** Site Bot pilot (10 clients, from 29.11). After a client pays, Eitan calls him for 10 minutes. This call is now REQUIRED before his site goes live. It replaces the typed questions that gave us too little unique material. The call is recorded (with his OK) and transcribed. Every sentence on his site must trace to something real he told us, so what he says becomes the substance of his pages.

**Persona:** an Israeli one-man local service business (plumber, electrician, AC tech, locksmith, etc.) with a Google profile and good reviews, usually no website. Busy, talks with his hands, hates marketing talk. He answers best with stories, not adjectives.

**What the script must get out of him (in this order of importance):**
1. One real recent job: what the customer called about, what he found, what he did, how long it took, roughly where.
2. What he refuses or refers on (jobs he won't take).
3. The question every customer asks on the phone, and his honest answer.
4. How he handles price: call-out fee? a "from" price for 1–3 common jobs? night/Shabbat rule? Or which promise: free quote / price confirmed before work / no hidden costs.
5. Local reality: the neighbourhoods or building types he works in most, and the local problem that keeps coming up.
6. Brands or equipment he works with, and any licence, certificate or authorised-supplier status.
7. His real response time, with its condition.
8. How a typical job runs, step by step.
Plus: a vague-answer probe. When he says "quality / reliable / professional", Eitan asks once for an example from the last month.

**Deliverables:**
- A. The call script for Eitan: opening (ask permission to record; explain in one line why: so the site sounds like him and only says true things), the questions with one follow-up each, and a closing. Spoken Hebrew, natural, short sentences. Eitan talks to him as a peer.
- B. A WhatsApp message Eitan sends after the call, asking for at least 3 real photos: his work (before/after allowed), his van or tools, and himself on the job if he's willing. Say why (real photos make the site his and get more calls) and what's not useful (stock images, screenshots, photos with customers' faces or house numbers).

**Constraints:** singular male; 12–15 words per sentence for anything spoken; no jargon (no "SEO", "content", "E-E-A-T"); no promises of rankings or results. Founder-facing: Noa reads it before Eitan.

**Output JSON:** {"callScript": {"opening": "...", "questions": [{"id": 1, "ask": "...", "followUp": "..."}], "vagueProbe": "...", "closing": "..."}, "photoRequestWhatsApp": "..."}
