- **What Site Bot is:** the complete organic Google exposure system for a one-man business whose main lead source is Google.
  - A core local website that mirrors the business's tree of services.
  - Built coherently with his Google Business Profile, so the two reinforce each other in the local results.
  - Plus getting the right written reviews: we help his customers leave them.
- **What it is NOT:** "a small website builder with easy updates". Easy updates and price are supporting points, never the headline.
