# Five new Dash ads: draft scripts for Noa (Fable 5.2, 2026-11-14)

Draft only: not yet through Noa, nothing edited, no API called.

**The split:** three Site Bot ads for the free check, two Ads Bot ads for "your ad and page before the first shekel". The fifth went to Site Bot because the free check is live today, while the Ads Bot pair must wait until the personal preview is public (the same hold as ST04 and ST07).

| # | Title | Product | Length | The joke in one line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | «היא התקשרה לאמבטיה» | Site Bot | 30s | His phone holds two thousand photos of his work; Google shows his bin. |
| 2 | «חנות פתוחה» | Site Bot | 15s | Everyone sees your fly is open and nobody tells you. Same on Google. |
| 3 | «המספר שלי» | Site Bot | 15s | The last "free check" made his phone ring for two weeks. This one never asks for the number. |
| 4 | «אני צריך לחשוב על זה» | Ads Bot | 30s | For once he is the customer who gets everything before paying. |
| 5 | «רק עמבה אין» | Ads Bot | 30s | His last campaign was shawarma in a sealed bag. A real stall builds it in front of you. |

Applies to all five:
- **Voice:** Dash speaks every line (Nimrod). No brand, founder name or price. The closing product name is the existing take.
- **Tags:** one per line, because the tag also picks Dash's face. For a one-take read, keep only the tags on the verdict line and the product line.
- **Lengths:** estimated from S1-15 (36 words, 18s) and A2-30 (63 words, about 35s).
- **Art:** no letters, digits or Google interface. A listing is a blank card with a pin, and a search is the magnifier.

## End cards

**Site Bot (ads 1–3).** The button stays «בדוק את העסק שלי בחינם». For these three I propose the stamp «מקלידים רק שם עסק. בלי הרשמה.» instead of «פיילוט ראשון: 10 עסקים בלבד.»:
- **These ads sell the free check.** "Only 10 businesses" under a free offer reads as if the check itself is limited, and it brings back the sales pressure the ad just removed.
- **No new Hebrew.** It is the first two sentences of the note already under the button on /site-bot, so the match with the page holds.
- **It proves the gift on mute.** Name only and no sign-up are the two facts that show it's no trap.
- **Fallback:** the pilot stamp still works with all three scripts if Eitan wants one card for every Site Bot ad. Keep it on the ads that sell the paid service (S1, ST13, ST20).

**Ads Bot (ads 4–5).** No change. Both product lines end on almost the same words as the stamp, so voice, card and button say one thing.

## Ranked: biggest hit first

1. **#1 «היא התקשרה לאמבטיה».** The opener is three words only this series can use, since the viewer is holding the character. Bathtub against bin reads on mute, the verdict is quotable, and the fix is the product's own advice (photos, from his phone, himself, free). It can air now.
2. **#4 «אני צריך לחשוב על זה».** The best last line of the five. It turns every tradesman's weekly grievance into his privilege, and it sells confidence: we build first.
3. **#5 «רק עמבה אין».** The closest relative of his favourite A2: the same opening shape, with Google in the first two seconds. It is the clearest by ear, and the stall's order of events is the product's.
4. **#2 «חנות פתוחה».** The most surprising opener and the tightest wordplay (open, opening hours, close it yourself). It ranks fourth only for taste risk.
5. **#3 «המספר שלי».** It answers the one objection the others skip, "what's the catch". It has the smallest laugh.

## Weakest lines

- **#2 line 2 is the weakest overall.** «ולֵךְ תדע» is a homograph that needs its niqqud to hold, and it is the softest claim in the set. This ad also puts the joke on him more than any approved ad does.
- **#1 line 2** leans on a Google behaviour (see flag 2). Line 4 is the flattest line, pure mechanics.
- **#3 line 1** asks the listener to work out that the ringing was sales calls.
- **#4 line 3** assumes he has paid for a campaign before. "Paid upfront" must be heard as "without seeing", never as a dig at advance billing, because Ads Bot is also paid monthly in advance.
- **#5 line 2:** nobody says what the falafel stands for (clicks instead of jobs). Line 3 is the longest breath, 18 words.

## Flags before production

1. **Ads 4 and 5 wait for the personal preview.** Today's public demo shows a sample business, so "your ad and your page" becomes true only when the preview is public. Also, the scripts say «כמה שאלות», and the questionnaire counts "question 1 of 25" (`src/app/(app)/google-ads/onboarding/page.tsx:153`). Either the air-time flow is short or that phrase changes.
2. **Ad 1, the street photo.** Line 2 says his listing showed a street photo because he had none. That is common on real listings, but it is a claim about Google, so Yonatan should confirm it with a dated source. If it fails, the half-line becomes «אצלך ריבוע אפור».
3. **"Seconds" versus "a minute".** The scripts and /site-bot say seconds; the audit page says a minute twice (`src/lib/site-bot/acquisitionCopy.ts:10-11`). The ads land on the audit page, so one of them should change.
4. **Recordings.** Site Bot needs nothing new: recorder shot S4 exists and the demo data already has zero photos (ad 1). Ad 2 needs opening hours switched off in the demo data. Ads Bot needs one new shot, the questionnaire ending on the ad and the page.
5. **«אני הטלפון שלך».** Dash has said "I" once before (A3), but never introduced himself. Two of the five open this way. If Eitan likes it, it can become the series opener; if not, ads 1 and 3 need a new first sentence.
6. **Rejected stories.** Their text is not in the repo, only their numbers, so I calibrated on the approved scripts and his recorded feedback.

## On the bench

- **"Mother" (Site Bot):** his mother walks in and in three seconds knows what's missing, free and unasked. The free check is that mother for his Google listing. This is the safest swap if #2 is too cheeky.
- **"Within the hour" (Ads Bot):** he sees his ad before paying; his wife reads "arrives within the hour" and notes he is two hours late to Friday dinner. It shows the ad is built from his own answers.
- **"The annual test" (Site Bot):** the van fails its yearly test on one burnt bulb he could have changed himself. The free check is the test before the test.
