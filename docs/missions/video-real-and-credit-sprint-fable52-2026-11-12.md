# Part 1. Video: real, HD, human

## Recommendation
**Hybrid "real hands, real screen".** Phone-quality-or-better footage of a real tradesman's hands,
tools, van and phone; the real product screen filmed on a real phone in a working hand; Nimrod's
voice; bold moving captions; the sound of the actual job under the voice. No face is required, nobody
speaks to camera, nobody is presented as a customer.

Why this one:
- The "AI look" comes from invented people and invented scenes. Real hands and a real phone remove it
  at almost no cost, and no AI-generated picture is needed anywhere.
- It keeps every founder rule: no name, one connected story, works with the screen off, price on the
  end screen only, Nimrod, WAO said upbeat.
- It avoids the two legal traps: testimonials (Ads Bot has no client results on file, ledger A34) and
  using a recognisable person without consent.
- One shoot day gives a footage bank for the 30-second ad, three situation shorts and cutaways for the
  explainer series.

Dash (illustrated) continues as the second track as already approved. Do not enlarge it.

## What AI is for, and what it is never for
| Use AI for | Never |
|---|---|
| Script drafts (waocopy), captions and their timing, transcript | A face, a body or a voice of a person who does not exist, shown as real |
| Colour, noise, stabilisation, upscaling of footage we filmed | A customer, a review, a testimonial, a star rating, a result |
| Nimrod's voice (founder's choice) | A generated work scene, house, van or street passed off as filmed |
| Rough cuts and shot selection | A Google screen, a Google logo, or a fake phone notification |
| Cleaning the location sound | A number that goes up, a "before and after" |

YouTube's disclosure page (read 2026-11-12) exempts captions, upscaling, repair, scripts and idea
generation, and requires disclosure for realistic AI content that shows a scene that did not happen.
It does not directly address a synthetic narrator; a label may appear for AI audio. With real footage
the picture needs no disclosure. Dror re-checks the upload boxes on the day.

## Shot list format
One row per narration line. The script decides the story; the table below is the format, filled with
the shot bank and placeholders until `ads-bot-real-30s-story` returns from Tamar and Noa.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `beat` | Script line ID (R01…). One line = one shot, except a hard cut at a marked midpoint. |
| `time` | Target in/out; real length comes from the audio. |
| `voice_ref` | Line ID in the approved script JSON. No Hebrew is retyped into the shot list. |
| `shot` | ID from the shot bank, or `NEW:` plus an English description. |
| `type` | `real` (filmed) · `screen-in-hand` (real product on a real phone) · `card` (text only) · `end`. |
| `framing` | 9:16 first; note the 16:9 crop. Safe zones as in the Site Bot intro spec. |
| `sound` | Location sound to keep under the voice (drill off, water, van door). |
| `caption` | `caption` field of the script line (four words or fewer). |
| `gate` | Claim gate that must pass for this line (GA1…GA5 from the intro mission), or none. |
| `release` | Whose release covers the shot. |
| `checks` | What stops the shot: a face, a number plate, a real name on screen, moiré on the phone. |

## Gaps found while reading (for Dror and the engineer; none is fixed here)
1. **Location.** Every campaign targets all of Israel (`create-campaign/route.ts:665-669`). The chosen area is saved to the config only (`:731`). A plumber in Haifa can pay for clicks from Eilat unless the keyword carries the town. Copy says nothing about area until fixed.
2. **Hours.** No ad schedule is set. Ads can run on days he does not work.
3. **Keyword text.** The strategy prompt asks for keywords written with brackets or quotes (`prompts.ts:422`); the route sends the text as is with phrase match (`create-campaign/route.ts:683-686`). Check that the symbols are stripped.
4. **"Price" is a default blocked word** (`prompts.ts:403`). People who search a trade plus "price" are often buyers. Dror to review.
5. **Prompt text out of date:** minimum budgets of ₪900–1,200 against the ₪1,500 in the terms (`prompts.ts:326-345`); a ₪9.9 payment button (`:376`); a click-ceiling formula (`:406`) against the fixed ₪15; "Manual CPC" for tight budgets (`:411`) against the code.
6. **Locksmiths** are sent to Local Services Ads (`prompts.ts:350`). Whether that product is available in Israel was not verified in this run.
7. **Calls from the ad's own call button are not counted** (`create-campaign/route.ts:261`).
8. **Clean-up is conditional.** A clean-up task is created only when spend runs ahead of pace, the cost ceiling is breached, or the digest suggests it (`operator.ts:386-413, 492-506`); the intent-mismatch rule needs a hand-made word list per client, which new clients do not have (`search-term-scoring.ts:195-197`).
9. **Autonomy can sit in shadow.** Live accounts act only if the legal terms version matches; otherwise the bot only proposes (`autonomy-consent.ts:32-39`). Any unconfirmed click protection blocks every action.
10. **The budget-increase action can never run:** it asks for +15% while the cap is the current budget (`autonomous-cycle.ts:200-204`; `autonomy-consent.ts:44`). Good for the promise, but the feature is inert.
11. **Sitelink Hebrew is fixed text in the plural** and was never proofread (`create-campaign/route.ts:233-244`).
12. **Step 0** is in the code; I did not verify that it is deployed, or that Google accepted an upload.
